{# These really are steps in order, so they're numbered #}
  1. On Saturday, print one copy for each centre and give it to the officials there.
  2. If the app stops working, officials tick people on paper and write the time. Phone the other centre about anyone you're unsure of.
  3. When the app works again, tick everyone from the paper in the app, so both centres' records join up.
  4. After voting, collect every copy: they hold members' personal details.
